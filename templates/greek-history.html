Hellenistic Period

Эллинистический
c.323 – 31 BC
  • Greek culture spreads to Egypt (Alexandria’s Library), Persia, and India;
  • Rome conquers Greece at 146 BCE, destruction of Corinth;
  • Rome defeats Cleopatra at 31 BCE; end of Hellenistic era
Rome conquers Greece, 146 BC

Classical Period

Классический
c.490 – 323 BC
  • Persian Wars – Greeks defeat Persians at Marathon;
  • Peloponnesian War – Sparta defeats Athens. Spartan dominance;
  • Theban rise after the Battle of Leuctra, 371 BCE;
  • Philip II of Macedon conquers Greece (Battle of Chaeronea, 338 BCE);
  • Alexander the Great conquers Persia, Egypt, and beyond at 336–323 BCE. At 323 BCE Alexander dies; empire splits into Ptolemaic Egypt, Seleucid Asia, and Antigonid Macedon;
  • Culture:
    • Parthenon built at 447–432 BCE;
    • Greek drama (Aeschylus, Sophocles, Euripides, Aristophanes);
    • Philosophy (Socrates, Plato, Aristotle).

Archaic Period

Архаика
c.800 – 490 BC
  • Colonization spreads Greeks across the Mediterranean and the Black Sea;
  • Rise of the polis: Athens, Sparta, Corinth, Thebes;
  • Greek alphabet develops from Phoenician script in c. 800 BCE;
  • Hesiod writes Theogony and Works and Days;
  • Athenian democracy established under Cleisthenes.

Dark Age

Темные века, Гомеровский период
c.1100 – c.800 BC
  • Decline of writing, monumental architecture, and centralized power;
  • Slavery appeares;
  • Early Iron Age. Iron replaces bronze;
  • Migrations (Dorian, Ionian) reshape the Greek population;
  • Epos appeares (Illiad, Odyssey).
Homer, c.800 BC

Minoan Civilization

c.2000 – c.1450 BCE

On Crete.

  • Large palace complexes (Knossos, Phaistos);
  • Art (frescoes, pottery) and undeciphered scripts (Linear A);
  • Possible matriarchal influences.

Mycenaean Civilization

c.1700 – c.1100 BCE

On mainland Greece.

  • Centers in Mycenae, Tiryns, Pylos;
  • Linear B script – early Greek language;
  • Trojan War legends emerge.
