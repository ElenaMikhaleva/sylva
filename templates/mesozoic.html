Triassic Period Триас

252 – 201 Mya

The Triassic is mostly a transitional recovery period between the desolate aftermath of the Permian Extinction and the lush Jurassic Period.

Early Triassic

252 – 247 Mya
  • The Early Triassic was a hot and arid epoch in the aftermath of the Permian Extinction.
  • Temnospondyli recovered first and evolved into large aquatic predators during the Triassic. Other reptiles also diversified rapidly, with aquatic reptiles such as ichthyosaurs and sauropterygians proliferating in the seas.
  • On land, the first true archosaurs appeared, including pseudosuchians (crocodile relatives) and avemetatarsalians (bird/dinosaur relatives).

Middle Triassic

247 – 237 Mya
  • Pangaea started to break up;
  • Phytoplankton, coral, crustaceans and many other marine invertebrates recovered from the Permian extinction;
  • On land, reptiles continued to diversify, conifer forests flourished, as well as the first flies.

Late Triassic

237 – 201 Mya
  • Warm and arid with a strong monsoon climate. A 2 million years-long wet season which transformed the arid continental interior into lush alluvial forests.
  • The first true dinosaurs appeared, and pterosaurs evolved a bit later.
  • Many large reptilian competitors to the dinosaurs were wiped out by the Triassic–Jurassic extinction event.

Jurassic Period Юра

201 – 145 Mya
  • Climate was humid, as a result, the world was warm and partially tropical, though possibly with short colder intervals. Conifer savannahs made up a large portion of the world's forests.
  • Plesiosaurs, ichthyosaurs and ammonites dominated the seas, while dinosaurs, pterysaurs and other reptiles dominated the land
  • True mammals were present during the Jurassic, but remained small.

Cretaceous Period Мел

145 – 66 Mya

The Early Cretaceous Epoch

145 – 100 Mya

Dinosaurs continued to be abundant, with groups such as tyrannosauroids, avialans (birds), marginocephalians, and ornithopods seeing early glimpses of later success. Other tetrapods, such as stegosaurs and ichthyosaurs, declined significantly

The Late Cretaceous Epoch

100 – 66 Mya
  • Climate became cooler. The tropical climate was restricted to the equator and areas beyond the tropic lines featured more seasonal climates.
  • The first flowering plants evolved.
  • Dinosaurs still thrived as new species such as Tyrannosaurus, Ankylosaurus, Triceratops and hadrosaurs dominated the food web.
  • Mammals diversified, with metatherians (marsupials and kin) and eutherians (placentals and kin) coming into their own.
  • In the oceans, mosasaurs diversified to fill the role of the now-extinct ichthyosaurs, alongside huge plesiosaurs such as Elasmosaurus.
  • At the end of the Cretaceous, volcanic eruptions were poisoning the atmosphere. As this was continued, it is thought that a large meteor smashed into Earth, creating the Chicxulub Crater and causing the event known as the K–Pg extinction, the fifth and most recent mass extinction event, during which 75% of life on Earth became extinct, including all non-avian dinosaurs. Every living thing with a body mass over 10 kilograms became extinct.
