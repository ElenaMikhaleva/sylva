Iliad

History

Dating

  • Written in 9th to 8th centuries BC;
  • Scientific study began in the 3rd century BC. It was also then that the division into 24 books was established, corresponding to the 24 letters of the Greek alphabet;
  • The earliest complete surviving text is Venetus A from 10th century.

Conditions surrounding the creation of the poems

  • The Trojan War was in 13th century BC. Homer lived centuries after the events described;
  • Not the only poem of its kind (Cypria about the causes of the Trojan War, other poems of the Trojan Cycle).
