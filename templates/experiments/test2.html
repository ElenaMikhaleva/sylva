Sociolinguistics — variation

Sept 3 · Linguistics

Labov's core finding: linguistic variation isn't random noise, it's socially structured. The same speaker shifts forms depending on formality, audience, and self-monitoring.

Key terms

  • Sociolect — variation tied to a social group rather than a region.
  • Prestige form — the variant associated with higher social status.
  • Covert prestige — a non-standard form valued within a group precisely because it isn't standard.

Question to revisit

How much of "code-switching" is conscious versus automatic? Look at Bell's audience design model next.

How it unfolded.

The geological time scale, oldest to most recent.

Cenozoic
66 million years ago – present

Mammals diversify and expand, grasslands spread, and humans emerge in the last few million years of this era.

Mesozoic
252 – 66 million years ago

Dinosaurs, early mammals, and flowering plants — ends with the asteroid impact and the extinction of the non-avian dinosaurs.

Paleozoic
539 – 252 million years ago

The Cambrian explosion, fish, the first plants and insects on land, ending with the largest mass extinction on record.

Precambrian
4.6 billion – 539 million years ago

Earth forms, an atmosphere develops, and the first single-celled life appears — the vast majority of Earth's history, with almost no fossil record.

Layer thickness is stylised, not to scale — the Precambrian alone spans roughly 88% of Earth's history.
