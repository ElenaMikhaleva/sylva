Precambrian Supereon.

Very little evidence survived, so most of its history is reconstructed from indirect evidence.

  • Earth forms and gradually cools;
  • Intense volcanic activity and frequent large asteroid impacts. Earth's surface is unstable, many volcanic eruptions, crust is recycled;
  • Moon forms (giant impact hypothesis);
  • Eon ends with the beginning of the Archean, when solid rocks formed.
