Hadean

Катархей
4567 – 4031 Mya

from Greek Hádēs (underworld)

Hadean Eon, illustration

Precambrian Supereon.

Very little evidence survived, so most of its history is reconstructed from indirect evidence.

  • Earth forms and gradually cools;
  • Intense volcanic activity and frequent large asteroid impacts. Earth's surface is unstable, many volcanic eruptions, crust is recycled;
  • Moon forms (giant impact hypothesis);
  • Eon ends with the beginning of the Archean, when solid rocks formed.

Archean

Архей
4031 – 2500 Mya

from Greek arkhḗ (beginning)

Precambrian Supereon.

Early crust stabilized and primitive life began.

  • Earth forms and gradually cools;
  • Intense volcanic activity and frequent large asteroid impacts. Earth's surface is unstable, many volcanic eruptions, crust is recycled;
  • Moon forms (giant impact hypothesis);
  • Eon ends with the beginning of the Archean, when solid rocks formed.
  • Atmosphere: methane, carbon dioxide, and nitrogene, no free molecular oxygen — unbreathable by nearly everything alive today:
    • methane contributed to the greenhouse effect and may have helped keep surface water liquid;
    • methane created ecosystem for methanotrophs.
  • Continental crust and protocontinents formed;
  • Stromatolites appeared — solid, domed structures built of thin layers of sediment by colonial cyanobacteria;
  • First life appeared (prokaryotes).
cyanobacteria, 3.2 to 2.7 Bya
Bacteria, 3.5 Bya
Archaea, 3.4 Bya

Proterozoic Eon

Протерозой
2500 – 538 Mya

from Greek próteros (earlier) + zōḗ (life)

Precambrian Supereon.

Atmospheric oxidation and evolution of complex cells.

  • Continents stabilize and grow (Kenorland, Columbia, Rodinia, then Pannotia);
  • Atmosphere: The Great Oxidation Event (GOE), oxygen accumulates for the first time, mainly because of photosynthesis by cyanobacteria;
  • Climate: oxygen reacted with methane that was a powerful greenhouse gas, stripping Earth's insulation and plunging the planet into global Ice Ages (Snowball Earth);
  • Species: oxygen was toxic to most existing anaerobic microbes, causing their extinction;
  • Species: eukaryotes appear;
  • Multi-cellular organisms appear (algae, Ediacaran fauna);
  • Eon ends with the beginning of the Phanerozoic, when abundant animal fossils appear.
GOE, 2.4 Bya
eukaryotes, 1.7 Bya
multi-cellular organisms, 1.6 Bya
