Ancient Culture by Losev:
  • Ancient culture is grounded in objectivism;
  • It relies on a material-sensory cosmologism, distinguishing it from medieval spiritual philosophies;
  • It features an animated-rational cosmologism;
  • The cosmos exists eternally by itself and acts as its own absolute, requiring no creator;
  • It manifests pantheism, where gods represent personified domains of nature, both rational and non-rational;
